Who we are

A way across.

Our name is khēyā (খেয়া), Bengali for a ferry crossing, borrowed from the title of Rabindranath Tagore’s 1906 book of poems Kheya.

For us, a ferry is a way across what separates where you are from where you need to be. That is our metaphor for helping you move from a question to a practical next step.

Questions along the way

You don’t need every answer to begin.

Do we need a complete project brief before talking?

No. Start with a business problem, a product idea or a system that needs to improve. The first job is to understand the work and decide what approach makes sense, including whether AI can help.

Can we start with a small project?

Yes. A defined feature, workflow or system upgrade makes it easier to agree on scope, test the result and decide what comes next. Advisory or a prototype can clarify the direction before a larger build.

How are cost and timing decided?

They depend on the scope, data, integrations and review requirements. Agree the deliverables, assumptions, responsibilities and acceptance criteria before committing to a build.

Do you only work on AI projects?

No. AI is one option, only where it fits the problem. Our services also cover technology decisions, custom software, modernization, automation, quality evaluation and delivery alongside your team. See Services for the areas we can explore together.

What happens after the first conversation?

We clarify the problem, dependencies and a manageable scope before agreeing any work. The first conversation helps both sides understand the next step; it does not commit you to an engagement.
