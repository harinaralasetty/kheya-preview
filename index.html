Before you begin

A few practical questions

You do not need to arrive with a technology choice. A business problem is enough to start.

Do we need a complete project brief before talking?

No. Start with a business problem, a product idea, or a system that needs to improve. The first job is to understand the work and decide what approach makes sense, including whether AI can help.

Can we start with a small project?

Yes. A defined feature, workflow, or system upgrade makes it easier to agree on scope, test the result, and decide what comes next. An advisory engagement or prototype can clarify the direction before a larger build.

How are cost and timing decided?

They depend on the scope, data, integrations, and review requirements. Agree the deliverables, assumptions, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria before committing to a build.

What should we share in a first conversation?

Describe the workflow, the tools involved, and what you want to improve. Keep confidential documents, customer information, and credentials out of the initial email. Data access and handling should be agreed before any project materials are shared.
