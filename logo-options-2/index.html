Minimal logo ideas

Every concept from the brainstorm (Codex, Claude Opus and Claude), 41 in all, in one orange (#C8692E, readable on both light and dark). Each is shown large, next to the name and at browser-tab sizes, plus as an app icon. Nothing is filtered out: from #18 on are the ones the first pass flagged (a lookalike, a letter, a loading spinner, weak at 16 px), kept so you can judge for yourselves. “Closest” notes are a visual check, not a trademark search. Starred ones made the top picks of the reviews. Pick a number. New sets below the numbered ones: Fill (F1–F22) and Teja’s feedback (T1–T14).

    Opus · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    A square cut by one curving gap: the river, with a shore on each side. The cleaner cousin of the split-bar reference.

    Closest: Split-square marks use straight cuts (Deutsche Bank is a straight slash in an outlined square).
