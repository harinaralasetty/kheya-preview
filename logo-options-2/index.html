Minimal logo ideas

Every concept from the brainstorm (Codex, Claude Opus and Claude), 41 in all, in one orange (#C8692E, readable on both light and dark). Each is shown large, next to the name and at browser-tab sizes, plus as an app icon. Nothing is filtered out: from #18 on are the ones the first pass flagged (a lookalike, a letter, a loading spinner, weak at 16 px), kept so you can judge for yourselves. “Closest” notes are a visual check, not a trademark search. Starred ones made the top picks of the reviews. Pick a number.

  1. 1River split★

    Opus · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    A square cut by one curving gap: the river, with a shore on each side. The cleaner cousin of the split-bar reference.

    Closest: Split-square marks use straight cuts (Deutsche Bank is a straight slash in an outlined square).

  2. 2Shifted shores★

    Opus · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    A circle split in two, the far half raised: getting from where you are to where you want to be.

    Closest: Nothing well known; sliced-coin marks exist in small companies.

  3. 3Lifted horizon★

    Codex · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    Half a sun above a shoreline that steps up toward the destination.

    Closest: Sun-at-horizon marks in general; the step makes it Kheya’s.

  4. 4Sunrise tile★

    Claude · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    An app-icon tile with the sun and the river cut out of it. Built to be an icon from day one.

    Closest: Weather-app and travel icons use sun-over-wave scenes; this one is cut out, not drawn on.
