Minimal logo ideas

Seventeen single-colour concepts in one orange (#C8692E, readable on both light and dark). Each is shown large, next to the name and at browser-tab sizes, plus as an app icon. Brainstormed with Codex and Claude Opus, then filtered: ideas that looked like a known logo, a letter, a loading spinner or clip-art were dropped. “Closest” notes are a visual check, not a trademark search. Starred ones made the top picks of the Codex, Opus or Claude reviews. Pick a number.

  1. 1River split★

    Opus · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    A square cut by one curving gap: the river, with a shore on each side. The cleaner cousin of the split-bar reference.

    Closest: Split-square marks use straight cuts (Deutsche Bank is a straight slash in an outlined square).

  2. 2Shifted shores★

    Opus · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    A circle split in two, the far half raised: getting from where you are to where you want to be.

    Closest: Nothing well known; sliced-coin marks exist in small companies.

  3. 3Lifted horizon★

    Codex · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    Half a sun above a shoreline that steps up toward the destination.

    Closest: Sun-at-horizon marks in general; the step makes it Kheya’s.

  4. 4Sunrise tile★

    Claude · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    An app-icon tile with the sun and the river cut out of it. Built to be an icon from day one.

    Closest: Weather-app and travel icons use sun-over-wave scenes; this one is cut out, not drawn on.

  5. 5Hull and sun★

    Opus · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    The ferry carrying you into the sunrise, in two shapes. Echoes the home page road.

    Closest: Generic ball-in-bowl shapes; can read as a bowl at a glance.

  6. 6River key★

    Codex · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    Two solid banks with a stepped river running between them; the crossing is the empty space.

    Closest: Distantly Houzz (block letter h); also reads a little like a maze.

  7. 7Between shores

    Codex · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    A dot between two banks: the ferry mid-river. Easiest of all to redraw from memory.

    Closest: Reads as a letter H with a dot; no well-known logo.

  8. 8Ferry between banks

    Claude · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    Two rounded shores facing each other across the water, with the ferry in the middle.

    Closest: Can read as a percent sign at small sizes.

  9. 9River eddies

    Codex · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    Two quarter-circles, like currents turning around open water.

    Closest: Nothing well known; very abstract, so the story needs telling.

  10. 10River sun

    Claude · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    A sun with a river running through it.

    Closest: Wave-split circles recall the Pepsi globe; one colour keeps it apart, but watch it.

  11. 11Sliced sun

    Opus · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    The sun on the waterline with its reflection below. The closest to Apple-level simplicity here.

    Closest: Sunset discs are common (travel, energy, retro graphics); hard to own alone.

  12. 12Rising arch

    Opus · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    A sun coming up under an arch: sunrise and gateway at once.

    Closest: Stock sunrise weather icons.

  13. 13Sunrise road

    Opus · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    A road narrowing to the horizon, where half a sun sits: the site’s road-to-sunrise story.

    Closest: Silhouette can read as a chess pawn or a lamp.

  14. 14Tidal fit

    Codex · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    One shore bulges where the other curves in, like a ferry finding its landing.

    Closest: Nothing well known; can read as a puzzle piece.

  15. 15Split K

    Claude · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    The split-bar reference turned into a K: a stem and two separate blades. Closest to today’s mark.

    Closest: Every K monogram (Kickstarter, Klarna); the detached arms help, but a K is hard to own.

  16. 16Summit flag

    Codex · lookalike risk medium

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    One raised pennant: the milestone flags the ship raises on the home page.

    Closest: Generic flag icons; the swallowtail is the only difference.

  17. 17Crossing blades, one colour

    Current · lookalike risk low

    Light KHEYA
    Dark KHEYA
    App icon

    Today’s mark in a single colour, for comparison.

    Closest: Already in use.
