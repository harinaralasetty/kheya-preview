Service 01

Technology strategy & advisory

Turn a business goal into a practical technology plan, with clear priorities, tradeoffs, and scope.

A useful fit when

You need to decide what to build, buy, modernize, or automate before committing time and budget.

Typical deliverables

  • A ranked shortlist of use cases, with reasons to pursue or defer each one
  • A build-versus-buy comparison with cost assumptions and dependencies
  • A feasibility and risk assessment for the selected workflow
  • A project brief with scope, ownership, and measurable acceptance criteria

What we need to understand

  • The business problem and how you measure it today
  • A walkthrough of the workflow and the tools involved
  • Budget, data-access constraints, and the people responsible for decisions

Service 02

Custom software development

Build websites, applications, platforms, and internal tools around the way your business works.

A useful fit when

Off-the-shelf tools do not fit your processes, or a product idea needs to become working software.

Typical deliverables

  • A product scope with user journeys, requirements, and acceptance criteria
  • Interface and architecture designs for the agreed application
  • Tested application code and integrations for the agreed scope
  • Deployment instructions, technical documentation, and handover materials

What we need to understand

  • The users, business goals, and core workflows
  • Existing designs, systems, or product requirements
  • Integration needs, operating constraints, and decision owners

Service 03

Digital transformation & modernization

Improve processes and update existing systems, using AI where it adds practical value.

A useful fit when

Legacy software, disconnected data, or manual processes make it difficult to adapt and grow.

Typical deliverables

  • A review of the current systems, processes, and dependencies
  • A prioritized modernization roadmap with migration risks and milestones
  • A scoped system upgrade, process redesign, or migration
  • Validation and handover plans for the systems and teams affected

What we need to understand

  • Current system documentation and a walkthrough of the work
  • Known bottlenecks, technical constraints, and business priorities
  • Data migration needs, system owners, and acceptable transition windows

Service 04

Automation & integrations

Connect repeatable work across your tools, with explicit limits and human approval where it matters.

A useful fit when

People spend time copying information, coordinating handoffs, or repeating the same steps across systems.

Typical deliverables

  • A workflow map and integration design for the agreed use case
  • A scoped automation connected to approved tools and systems
  • Approval steps, action logs, and a plan for retries and failures
  • Tests for normal and failure cases, plus handover documentation

What we need to understand

  • Examples of the work, including exceptions and failed handoffs
  • Documentation for the systems that need to connect
  • Rules for what the system may do and what a person must approve

Service 05

AI & intelligent systems

Apply AI to search, document processing, assistants, and decision support, with clear quality checks.

A useful fit when

You have a task involving language, documents, or complex information that could benefit from AI.

The approach depends on the task. Document search may need source citations; an assistant may need action limits and approvals. Data quality, access rules, and evaluation shape the design.

Typical deliverables

  • A use-case assessment, data review, and design for the agreed AI system
  • A scoped AI prototype, with source references where the use case requires them
  • Data access controls, update handling, and human review steps
  • An evaluation set for output quality, failure cases, and unsupported claims

What we need to understand

  • Representative data or documents approved for the project
  • Examples of the task and what a useful result looks like
  • Data owners, access rules, and review requirements

Service 06

Production quality & evaluation

Test software and AI systems against agreed requirements, and prepare them for release and daily operation.

A useful fit when

A prototype or existing system needs clearer tests, release criteria, or operational ownership.

Release criteria should reflect how the system will be used. Testing, monitoring, and ownership are agreed for the project, including AI evaluation where relevant.

Typical deliverables

  • A risk assessment tied to the system and its intended use
  • Tests for functionality, reliability, access controls, and relevant AI failure cases
  • Review and release checklists with named decision owners
  • A monitoring and incident-response plan with escalation steps

What we need to understand

  • The system design, intended users, and planned uses
  • Existing policies and relevant requirements from your advisers
  • Known failure cases and the people responsible for review

Service 07

Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)

Engineers working alongside a client’s team to integrate and deliver solutions within its real workflows.

A useful fit when

A solution needs to fit the way our team actually works. Requirements, integrations and acceptance become clearer through work with the team in its real workflow.

Typical deliverables

  • Workflow and integration map
  • scoped work plan with responsibilities and acceptance criteria
  • working integrations or features
  • evidence from agreed workflow checks
  • documentation and handover materials for the work delivered

What we need to understand

  • A workflow owner and participating team
  • representative tasks and exceptions
  • system owners/documentation
  • explicitly approved access and data-handling rules
  • decision, acceptance and operating responsibilities
